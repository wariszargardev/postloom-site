Plan posts on a calendar. Publish them on time.

Postloom is a personal, self-hosted social media scheduler built by Muhammad Waris. It runs on the owner's own computer and posts only to the owner's own accounts, channels and pages. It is free, has no ads, and nobody else can sign up.

Supported platforms

  • LinkedIn (profile and company pages)
  • YouTube
  • Facebook Pages
  • Instagram (professional accounts)
  • TikTok
  • Threads
  • Bluesky

What it does

01 Schedule

Write a post once, pick the accounts and a time on the calendar, and adjust the text and settings for each platform.

02 Publish

At the chosen time Postloom uploads the text, photos or video to each connected account through the platform's official API.

03 Read stats

After publishing it reads the post's own view, like, comment and share counts, so the owner can see what worked.

How accounts are connected

Each account is connected with the platform's own sign-in screen (OAuth). Postloom never sees a password. It keeps the access token the platform gives it, encrypted, and uses it only to publish the owner's posts and read their statistics. Access can be removed at any time, in Postloom or in the platform's own settings.
