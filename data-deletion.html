Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Postloom keeps only what it needs to publish posts and show their statistics: an encrypted access token, the account's id, name and picture, the posts, and their stats. Here is how to remove Postloom's access and have that data deleted.

1. Remove Postloom's access on the platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: on Facebook open Settings and privacy, Settings, then Business integrations (or Apps and websites). Find Postloom, choose Remove, and confirm. Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites.
  • Threads: Settings, Account, Website permissions, then remove Postloom.
  • YouTube / Google: open myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Postloom.
  • TikTok: in the app, Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, then remove Postloom.
  • LinkedIn: Settings, Data privacy, Permitted services, then remove Postloom.
  • Bluesky: Settings, Privacy and security, App passwords, then delete the one made for Postloom.

Once access is removed, Postloom can no longer post or read anything on that account.

2. Ask for Postloom's copy to be deleted

  1. Email wariszargardev@gmail.com with the subject "Postloom data deletion request".
  2. Say which platform and which account (name, handle or page) the data belongs to.
  3. The account is disconnected in Postloom and its token, profile details, posts and statistics are deleted within 30 days. Backups that still hold them roll off after 14 more days.
  4. You get a reply by email when the deletion is done.

If you are the owner

In Postloom open Accounts and choose Disconnect on the account. Postloom stops using it at once, and the nightly clean-up deletes its data permanently after 30 days.
