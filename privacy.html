Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Postloom is a personal, self-hosted social media scheduler built and run by Muhammad Waris. It runs on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own accounts and channels. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign up. This policy explains what data Postloom handles, why, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

Who is responsible

Muhammad Waris, Pakistan. Contact: wariszargardev@gmail.com.

The data Postloom handles

  • Access tokens. When an account is connected with the platform's sign-in screen (OAuth), the platform gives Postloom an access token and, for most platforms, a refresh token. They are stored encrypted. Postloom never sees or stores a password.
  • Profile details. The connected account's id, name or handle, and profile picture, to show which account a post goes to.
  • Posts. The text, photos, videos and settings the owner writes and uploads in Postloom, and the time each one is scheduled.
  • Statistics. After a post is published, its own counts (views, likes, comments, shares and similar numbers) and the post's link, so the owner can see how it did.
  • Publishing history. When each post was sent, whether it worked, and the platform's error message if it failed.

Postloom does not collect data about other people, does not read private messages, and does not use cookies or trackers on this website. The Postloom app itself only uses a session cookie to keep its owner signed in.

How the data is used

  • Only to provide Postloom's features to its owner: scheduling, publishing, statistics and editing the owner's own posts.
  • It is never sold, never shared with advertisers or data brokers, never used for advertising, and never used to build profiles of people.

Where the data is kept

  • In a MySQL database on the owner's own computer. Tokens are encrypted. Uploaded photos and videos are files on the same computer.
  • Daily database backups are kept next to it for 14 days.

When data leaves Postloom

  • To the platform being posted to, to publish the owner's post or read its statistics, through that platform's official API.
  • To an AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude), only when the owner uses an AI feature such as writing a caption or checking a thumbnail. Only the post text, title, tags or image needed for that feature is sent. Tokens are never sent.
  • To the owner's own alert channels (email, Telegram or Slack, if the owner turns them on), for example a caption to paste or a note that a post failed.
  • Technical logs about jobs and errors may go to a logging service. They never contain tokens or passwords.
  • Instagram and Threads fetch a photo from a short-lived signed link to the owner's computer while a post is published. That link only serves that one file and expires within minutes.

Google and YouTube data

When a YouTube channel is connected, Google asks for these permissions:

  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile): to show which Google account a channel belongs to.
  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos the owner schedules, with their title, description, tags, privacy setting and thumbnail.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's name, picture and subscriber count, and its recent uploads with their view, like and comment counts, for statistics and the channel audit.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to change a video's title, description and tags only when the owner clicks Apply to YouTube (and put the old details back with Undo), and to add a comment the owner wrote under the owner's own video.

Limited Use. Postloom's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Postloom uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to the data Google holds.

You can remove Postloom's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Meta data (Facebook, Instagram and Threads)

  • Facebook Pages: pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_read_user_content and read_insights, to list the owner's Pages, publish to them, and read each published post's reactions, comment and share counts, and views and reach. Postloom keeps the Page's id, name and Page access token.
  • Instagram professional accounts: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, instagram_manage_insights, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management, to find the Instagram account linked to the owner's Page, publish to it, and read each post's like and comment counts, views, reach and saves. Postloom keeps the account's id, username and picture.
  • Threads: threads_basic, threads_content_publish and threads_manage_insights, to publish and read each post's views, likes, replies, reposts and quotes.

You can remove Postloom from your Facebook account under Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations (and Apps and websites). How to have Postloom's copy deleted is on the Data deletion page.

TikTok data

  • user.info.basic and user.info.profile: the account's open id, display name, username and avatar.
  • video.upload and video.publish: to send the owner's video to TikTok, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a direct post with the privacy, comment, Duet, Stitch and disclosure settings the owner chose. Before a direct post, Postloom asks TikTok which privacy options and video length the account allows.
  • video.list (only if turned on): each of the owner's videos' id, title, description, date, link, and view, like, comment and share counts, for statistics.

You can remove Postloom's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.

LinkedIn and Bluesky

  • LinkedIn: openid, profile, email and w_member_social to post to the owner's profile; w_organization_social, r_organization_social and rw_organization_admin to post to company pages the owner manages.
  • Bluesky: signed in with a handle and an app password. The app password is not saved; only the session tokens Bluesky returns are kept, encrypted.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept while an account is connected and used.
  • Disconnecting an account in Postloom stops all use of it at once. Its data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Backups that still hold it roll off after 14 more days.
  • To have Postloom's copy of your data deleted sooner, email wariszargardev@gmail.com. Steps: Data deletion.

Security

Postloom runs on its owner's computer and is not open to the public: from the internet only short-lived signed media links and, when the owner turns it on, the owner's own sign-in page can be reached. Tokens are encrypted in the database, and connections to the platforms use https.

Children

Postloom is not meant for, and does not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
